# Privacy Policy for YouTube Time Spoiler Blocker

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-04

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Our extension is designed with privacy in mind. 
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Local Storage:** User settings (such as the spoiler-free threshold and on/off status) are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage`). This data never leaves your device.
- **No Browsing History:** While the extension interacts with YouTube's UI, it does not track, record, or share your browsing history.

## 2. Permissions Justification
- **storage:** Used only to save your preference settings locally.
- **host permissions (*://*.youtube.com/*):** Used only to inject the spoiler-prevention toggle and modify the progress bar on YouTube.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. No data is collected for advertising or tracking purposes.

## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Chrome Web Store support page.
